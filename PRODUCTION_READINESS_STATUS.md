## Bottom Line

**Updated 2026-08-15:** One of the two original Critical, launch-blocking gaps is now closed — security headers are live on `nginx.frontend.conf`. **Zero automated test coverage remains the one standing Critical blocker.** The admin-token `sessionStorage` gap (previously ⚠️ Partially Done) is also now fully fixed, backed by a real backend refresh/logout flow. Several other Critical/High items from the original audit were already genuinely fixed (Dockerfile, onboarding PII slip-through gate, dashboard silent-zero bug, attachment upload atomicity), and one big architectural shift (SSE) closed a whole category of findings. But `debugError` leakage and the unpartialized onboarding store — both flagged as release blockers — are still exactly as found. TLS/HSTS was tried and then deliberately reverted 2026-08-15 — a self-signed cert added no real protection for pure-loopback traffic while costing a browser trust-warning; revisit if this ever becomes reachable over a real network. Net: real, continued progress — the last structural blocker standing between this app and launch-ready is test coverage.
